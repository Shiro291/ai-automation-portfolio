## 3. Extracting from your CV: The "Ethical Exaggeration" Guide
We must abide by the `marketing-psychology` Ethical Guardrails (No false statements, but aggressive framing). We will focus on *outcomes and methodologies* rather than simple "student duties".

### How to frame your Education / Teaching Experience:
* **Your Degree (PGSD - Primary School Teacher Education):** 
  * *Instead of:* "Studying to be a primary school teacher."
  * *Write:* "Specialized in Pedagogical Architectures & Cognitive Development Psychology. Focused on researching how digital environments influence knowledge retention in early education."
* **Teaching Practice / PPL / Micro-Teaching:** 
  * *Instead of:* "Student Teacher / Asisten Guru."
  * *Write:* "Curriculum Implementer & Educational Facilitator." 
  * *Impact to highlight:* "Designed and executed immersive lesson plans (e.g., 'Angkasa Explorer Space Station Simulation'), managing real-time student engagement and evaluating learning metrics against national curriculum standards."
* **Technical Software Engineering (RPL History):**
  * *Instead of:* "Learned coding in vocational high school."
  * *Write:* "Foundational background in Software Engineering, building a deep understanding of core programming logic, database structuring, and UI/UX design before transitioning to apply these skills to EdTech."

### What NOT to do:
* Don't list random non-relevant courses.
* Don't use weak verbs (e.g., "Helped with...", "Learned about..."). Use strong action verbs (e.g., "Architected", "Engineered", "Facilitated", "Analyzed").
